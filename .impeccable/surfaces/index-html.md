# Surface: Business × Organization Atlas (whole app)

Scope: the whole single-page app — all ten views, the two interactive instruments (Bridge and Forces), and the shared shell (nav, brand seal, footer). Mode: **Operate** — the visitor completes a task (choices → organizational form), so the visual world contributes type, palette, density, and one signature move while standard web controls stay standard.

Audience: founders, operators, managers, consultants, and students. Job: move from business-model choices to a concrete organizational-form recommendation and understand why.

Must stay untouched: all content and copy, the ten views, the Bridge and Forces interaction logic, the six languages, the CSP / no-build / no-dependency constraints, and the audit contract.

Chosen direction: structural engineering · mechanics language — business model as loads, organizational forms as frames, Bridge as structural selection, forces as stresses, fit as equilibrium. Signature move: the Bridge "structural resolution" diagram; the Forces radial vector field is the secondary move in the same grammar.

Memorable moment: watching four business choices resolve live into a ranked set of structural frames, the best fit settling into signal-red equilibrium.

Unresolved decisions: none blocking — the direction was user-pinned and the build is complete.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Replace the card metaphor with structural selection. The business model is the load; the organizational form is the frame; Bridge is the selection that matches loads to frames; fit is equilibrium. The category default this refuses is the "cards on a light SaaS studio" arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Cool drafting paper (#e7eaee) with a faint grid, near-black drafting ink, hairline rules and line-weight depth instead of soft shadows, dual accents — signal red #b02a1e (loads / tension) and structural steel blue #1f5c8a (members / fit) — true monospace (JetBrains Mono) for labels and data, a serif display (Archivo) for headings, near-square 4px corners, tabular numerals, and a square "seal" brand mark.

STORY: The visitor understands their business as a set of loads, sees those loads resolve into candidate frames, and reads the equilibrium as a concrete, explained recommendation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The Bridge view opens as the active tab: four business-choice groups on the left (load inputs), the result panel on the right holding the structural-resolution diagram — load arrows feeding a reaction member that distributes to three ranked frames, the primary frame settling in signal red — with the fit state and reasoning below.

FORM: A user-pinned direction (structural engineering · mechanics language), one of four presented directions and chosen by the user. seed: none (user-pinned, no roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
